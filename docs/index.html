Scorly

SEO Score & OG Preview

A free, open source browser extension that scores any page's on-page SEO in depth, shows how it will look when shared, and diffs the version you're building against the one that's already live.

Click the icon and Scorly reads the page you're already on — no URL to paste, no account, no subscription. The analysis runs locally in your browser, and nothing is sent to a remote server. It works on localhost and private-network URLs too, where most SEO tools refuse to run.

What it checks

  • An overall score from 0–100 across 8 categories — Technical, Content, Performance, Schema, Security, Mobile, AI Visibility and E-E-A-T — each with a checklist of what passed, what needs attention and what failed
  • Meta tags, readability and keyword statistics, heading structure, internal and external links, image alt text, JSON-LD schema, technology signals, TTFB, request count, transfer size, and security checks
  • How the page is likely to appear when shared, with a fallback preview built from the page content when Open Graph tags are missing
  • A word-by-word diff of any two pages — live, staging or localhost — exportable as PDF, CSV, JSON or Markdown

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no remote server. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and what requests are made.
